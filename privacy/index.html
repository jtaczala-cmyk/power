Privacy

Last updated 30.09.2026

No cookies

The game uses no cookies and no analytics tools.

Data stays on your device

Your name, results (points and KO), high score and settings (e.g. sound) are stored only in your browser’s storage (localStorage) on your device. The game files are also kept in the browser cache so the game works offline.

Nothing is sent to the developer

The game sends no data to the developer. The leaderboard only shows results saved on this device.

Hosting

The game is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When pages are loaded, GitHub may log your IP address and technical browser data, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement. Fonts are hosted with the game; no connection to Google Fonts is made.

Deleting data

You can delete this data at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings.
