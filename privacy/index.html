Privacy

Last updated 30.09.2026

Cookie-free statistics

The game uses no cookies. Visits are counted anonymously with GoatCounter (script hosted with the game, data on servers in Finland/Germany): page views, referring page, browser and system, language, screen width, country (derived from the IP address) and the events “game start” and “game end”. GoatCounter stores no IP address or user ID and leaves nothing in your browser, so you cannot be identified from it. Details: GoatCounter privacy policy.

Data on your device

Your nickname, results (points and KO), high score and settings (e.g. sound) are stored in your browser’s storage (localStorage) on your device. The game files are also kept in the browser cache so the game works offline.

Online leaderboard

If you enter a nickname, the following is sent to the online leaderboard after a game: nickname, result (points, KO, wave) and the time it was saved. This is stored in a Supabase database (Supabase, Inc.) in the EU region (Ireland) and is publicly visible on the leaderboard. No personal data is required – please don’t use your real name or e-mail address. Entering a nickname is optional; without one your result stays on your device. Supabase may technically log IP addresses in its server logs (Supabase privacy policy); the game does not store them on the leaderboard.

Hosting

The game is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When pages are loaded, GitHub may log your IP address and technical browser data, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement. Fonts are hosted with the game; no connection to Google Fonts is made.

Deleting data

You can delete the data on your device by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings. To have an entry removed from the online leaderboard, e-mail https://github.com/jtaczala-cmyk/power/issues (with the nickname, game and approximate date).
